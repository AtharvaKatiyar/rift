Anatomy of a link

Two parts. One that's yours, one that's permanent.

rift.dpdns.org/portfolio/A7XK29M4PQ8L
DomainFixed. The same for every task, every user.
SlugYours to choose — memorable, and specific to this one task.
Public IDA random 12-character string. Guarantees your link is yours alone.
One link per task

Every task gets its own link. Every link is independent.

Updating where your portfolio link points has zero effect on your resume link, your startup link, or anything else you've created. Watch each one change on its own.

Portfolio

Update any destination inside Rift and every copy of that one link — on a resume, a QR code, an old email — resolves to the new destination immediately.

Built around one job: keep the link, change what's behind it.

Every feature exists to make that guarantee solid, even years and dozens of updates later.

One link, one task

Each link you create is scoped to a single purpose and fully independent of every other link in your account.

Update anytime

Swap a link's destination as often as you need. The public URL never changes, no matter how many times you edit it.

Custom slugs

Choose a slug that names the task — /portfolio, /resume, /startup — Rift adds a unique public ID automatically.

Reserved-route protection

Slugs like login, admin, and api are protected, so your links can never collide with the platform itself.

Fast, every time

Links resolve quickly and consistently, whether they get five clicks a month or five thousand.

Full control, one dashboard

Create, update, deactivate, or reactivate any of your links from a single place, any time you need to.
